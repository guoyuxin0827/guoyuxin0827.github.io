Crane Systems / 01

Active Inference LQR Crane Control

Anti-sway and positioning control for crane systems based on active inference and optimal control.

Background

Crane systems are underactuated and strongly coupled. Payload oscillation can reduce transportation efficiency and make accurate positioning difficult.

Main Work

  • Established the dynamic model of the crane system.
  • Designed an active inference based control framework.
  • Integrated LQR into the controller design.
  • Evaluated positioning and anti-sway performance.
MATLAB Control Theory Active Inference LQR
